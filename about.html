Dr. Rajesh Srivastava

Former President, IDA Agra (twice) · Central Council Member, IDA

A pioneer of dentistry in Agra. He has led the Indian Dental Association Agra as President twice, served on the Central Council at the IDA head office, and has been awarded several times as one of the most active IDA members in U.P.

In 1986 he single-handedly started the dental department at Saran Ashram Hospital, Dayalbagh, under the supreme guidance of Gracious Huzur Dr. M.B. Lal Sahab — and still heads it today, on an honorary basis.

Dr. Punit Srivastava

MDS · Endodontist · Implantologist · Director, SMDC

BDS from Saraswati Dental College, Lucknow (2008) and MDS in Conservative Dentistry & Endodontics from Divya Jyoti College, Modinagar (2014). With almost 17 years of clinical experience, he directs SMDC — one of the foremost dental clinics in Agra.

His speciality is painless, single-sitting root canal treatment, with micro-endodontics and surgery under his own Labomed microscope. Carrying on the legacy, he also serves at Saran Ashram Hospital, Dayalbagh, on an honorary basis.

NO SILLY QUESTIONS

Everything you're
afraid to ask.

The stuff people whisper about dentists — answered honestly, no sugar-coating.

Will it hurt?
We use modern anesthesia and go entirely at your pace. Most patients say the tiny numbing pinch is the worst part — and you can raise your hand to pause anytime.
How much will it cost — really?
You get the full cost upfront, in writing, before anything begins. No surprise bills and no "while we're in there" add-ons.
What if I haven't seen a dentist in years?
No lectures and no judgement. We meet you exactly where you are and build a plan you're comfortable with — one step at a time.
Do I actually need this treatment?
If you don't, we'll tell you so. We'd rather keep you as a patient for life than sell you something you don't need today.
I'm really anxious. Can you help?
Absolutely — just tell us when you book. We'll give you extra time, explain every step, and never, ever rush you.
Will I be kept waiting?
We run on time. Book a slot and you'll be seen close to it — not an hour later in a crowded waiting room.
