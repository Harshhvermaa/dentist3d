NO SILLY QUESTIONS

Everything you're
afraid to ask.

The stuff people whisper about dentists — answered honestly, no sugar-coating.

Will it hurt?
We use modern anesthesia and go entirely at your pace. Most patients say the tiny numbing pinch is the worst part — and you can raise your hand to pause anytime.
How much will it cost — really?
You get the full cost upfront, in writing, before anything begins. No surprise bills and no "while we're in there" add-ons.
What if I haven't seen a dentist in years?
No lectures and no judgement. We meet you exactly where you are and build a plan you're comfortable with — one step at a time.
Do I actually need this treatment?
If you don't, we'll tell you so. We'd rather keep you as a patient for life than sell you something you don't need today.
I'm really anxious. Can you help?
Absolutely — just tell us when you book. We'll give you extra time, explain every step, and never, ever rush you.
Will I be kept waiting?
We run on time. Book a slot and you'll be seen close to it — not an hour later in a crowded waiting room.
